# Changelog

What changed in each release, for the people using the app. The version is in `VERSION` and at the foot of the
account menu; releasing is in [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#each-release). Versions follow
[semantic versioning](https://semver.org): a new feature raises the middle number (1.1.0), a fix the last (1.0.1).

## Unreleased

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

The first numbered release: the app as it is today.

- Import a recipe from a link, a YouTube video, screenshots or its text. Claude tidies it up, and it waits in the
  Draft box to be reviewed and saved. Recipes can also be typed in by hand.
- Each recipe has its own icon, chosen beside its name, or a colourful placeholder. Tags are names only.
- Browse recipes by tag from the sidebar, or search by name, ingredient or source. Add tags of your own.
- Plan the week's meals, and get the grocery list for free, grouped by aisle in shopping order. Items you already
  have stay ticked off.
- Ask the assistant (✦) what to cook: it recommends from your own recipes, imports links you paste, and remembers
  preferences such as "we're vegetarian".
- Share a recipe by link. Sign-up is by invitation. The app is in English and Chinese.
